How the recommendation is calculated
Rules

Reorder point = forecast demand during supplier lead time + safety stock.

Order-up-to level = forecast demand during lead time and review period + safety stock.

Suggested quantity = order-up-to level − net available stock, rounded up and never below zero. Confirm that the backend uses these same rules. This starter implementation does not enforce supplier pack sizes, minimum order quantities, shelf life, or budget limits.
